# Lead.AI Review Sentinel (`arun-gharami/lead-ai-review-sentinel`)

The **Lead.AI Review Sentinel** is an NLP sentiment analysis and customer review anomaly detection model engineered to monitor brand feedback, detect fake/spam reviews, and analyze customer sentiment trends. Developed by **Lead.AI Labs** ([www.lead-ai.us](https://www.lead-ai.us)).

## 💼 Business Use Cases

1. **Automated Customer Support Routing:** Instantly flag negative reviews and route urgent complaints to support agents.
2. **Review Fraud & Spam Detection:** Detect unnatural sentiment velocity spikes and synthetic feedback.
3. **Product Feedback Analytics:** Extract key product pain points from unstructured customer text.
