## 🏢 About Lead.AI Labs

**Lead.AI Labs** is a specialized artificial intelligence engineering and research lab dedicated to building explainable, trustworthy, and high-performance AI solutions for modern businesses. Founded by **Arun Kumar Gharami**, Lead.AI Labs bridges the gap between complex machine learning research and practical, high-ROI business applications.

We specialize in **Explainable AI (XAI)**, **predictive analytics**, **fraud detection**, and **decision-support automation** designed for fintech, e-commerce, payment processing, and enterprise automation workflows.

## 🛠️ What We Build

* **Explainable Risk Scoring Systems:** Transparent ML models that provide precise fraud probabilities alongside human-interpretable feature contribution summaries.
* **Predictive Customer Intelligence:** Machine learning models for customer lifetime value, churn prevention, and behavioral segmentation.
* **Business Process Automation:** Autonomous workflow pipelines that combine custom AI logic with enterprise APIs.
* **Trustworthy Open AI Assets:** Benchmark datasets, reproducible model cards, and interactive web demos for the global AI community.

---

## 🛡️ Featured Product: Lead.AI Fraud Shield

**Lead.AI Fraud Shield** is an explainable fraud detection and risk-scoring AI system designed specifically for small businesses, fintech teams, e-commerce stores, payment review teams, and AI automation clients.

Key Capabilities:
* Real-time transaction risk scoring (0–100%).
* Explainable AI (XAI) feature impact breakdowns explaining *why* a transaction was flagged.
* Configurable risk thresholds (Low, Medium, High).
* Modular API and Gradio interface integration.

## 💼 Business Use Cases

1. **Fintech & Payment Gateway Risk Scoring:** Pre-screening transactions before authorization to reduce chargeback ratios.
2. **E-Commerce Checkout Security:** Detecting velocity anomalies, proxy device usage, and high-risk merchant categories.
3. **Manual Review Automation:** Assisting human risk analysts by highlighting exact risk factors in transaction review queues.
4. **Custom Enterprise AI Integration:** Embedding open-weight or custom-trained risk models into existing cloud microservices.

## ⚖️ Responsible AI Statement

Lead.AI Labs is committed to ethical AI development. Our risk-scoring models are designed as **decision-support systems** and should always be paired with human oversight, continuous monitoring, and domain validation when deployed in financial environments. We do not use unconsented PII or real private banking data; all public demonstration datasets are synthetic.
