## 📋 What Manual Steps Remain

1. **Hugging Face Org Card Copy-Paste:** Copy the text from `org-card/README.md` and paste it into the organization profile editor on [huggingface.co/lead-ai-labs](https://huggingface.co/lead-ai-labs).
2. **Execute Deployment Commands:** Run the scripts in `upload_commands.md` using your write token (`export HF_TOKEN="your_actual_token"`).
3. **Verify Dataset Viewer:** Open both dataset pages on Hugging Face to confirm that the tabular viewer renders `train.csv` and `sample_data.csv`.
4. **Create HF Collections:** Group your HF assets into the 4 planned collections per `collections/collection-plan.md`.
5. **Publish LinkedIn Announcement:** Post the launch copy from `launch/LINKEDIN_POST.md`.
